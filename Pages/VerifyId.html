Identity Verification

Before you can participate in marketplace transactions, we need to verify your identity.

Why do we verify your identity?

MarketView is designed to allow users to exchange goods through transactions supported by an applied escrow process. Because some transactions may involve users meeting to exchange goods, we need to establish the identity of the people participating in the marketplace.

  • Helps ensure that marketplace participants are genuine and identifiable.
  • Helps create a safer environment when buyers and sellers arrange physical meet-ups.
  • Supports trust during escrow-protected transactions involving the exchange of goods.

Your identity verification is used to support marketplace safety and transaction integrity.
